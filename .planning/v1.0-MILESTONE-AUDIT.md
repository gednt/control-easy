## Headline finding

**This is a mid-milestone state, not a complete milestone.** The project is at Phase 8 of 14 ("Ready to plan"). Phases 8–10 (Design System, UI Parity, Dashboard) and Phase 13 (Continuous Engineering) are not started — together they cover 13 of 36 v1 requirements (UI-01–05, DASH-01–04, CI-01–04). The milestone cannot be archived with 36% of its requirements unsatisfied and unstarted.

On top of that, the project has a **verification gap**: 6 of the 7 phases claimed complete in `STATE.md` (Phases 1–6) were imported into GSD state without producing per-phase GSD verification artifacts (CONTEXT.md / PLAN.md / SUMMARY.md / VERIFICATION.md). Only Phase 7 has the full set. Phase 11 (Demo Mode) was implemented and reconciled (2026-07-12) outside the GSD lifecycle entirely. The 3-source cross-reference rule in the audit workflow therefore marks 22 already-"complete" requirements as **orphaned** — they are claimed in `REQUIREMENTS.md` and `STATE.md` but absent from every phase `VERIFICATION.md` because no such file exists for their phase.

The code itself is largely healthy (integration sampling found 5 of 8 E2E flows intact, with the known fragility around dashboard stats + tenant-filter JOINs documented in `CONCERNS.md`). The gap is **process and verification discipline**, not architectural breakage. The path forward is to close the in-flight phases (8–10, 13) and either backfill GSD verification artifacts for Phases 1–6 + 11 or accept them as pre-GSD legacy.
